About Our Coffee House

Founded in 2018 by two Miami University alumni, Maple & Bean Coffee House was born out of a passion for great coffee and community gathering spaces. Located in the heart of Oxford, Ohio, we pride ourselves on creating a warm, comfortable haven where students, faculty, and locals feel right at home.

We proudly partner with Midwest Craft Roasters for ethically sourced, locally roasted beans, and receive fresh daily baked goods from our neighbors at High Street Bakery.

Why You'll Love It Here

  • Free High-Speed Wi-Fi: Outlets accessible at every table for study sessions.
  • Cozy & Spacious Seating: Plush armchairs, large worktables, and quiet nooks.
  • Vegan & Dairy-Free Options: Oat, almond, and soy milk plus gluten-free pastries.
  • Student & Faculty Discount: 10% off your entire order with a valid ID.
